Bird Photo Editor
=================

Bird Photo Editor runs entirely on your computer. It does not upload photos,
metadata or usage data. It is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

This program includes the following third-party software (licences in the
installed files or at the listed projects):

- Python (PSF License)
- LibRaw via rawpy (LibRaw: LGPL-2.1 or CDDL-1.0; rawpy: MIT)
- NumPy (BSD-3-Clause), OpenCV (Apache-2.0), Pillow (MIT-CMU),
  tifffile (BSD-3-Clause), ExifRead (BSD-3-Clause), piexif (MIT)
- FastAPI, Starlette, Uvicorn, Pydantic (MIT / BSD-3-Clause)
- ONNX Runtime (MIT)
- React (MIT)
- Optional bird detection models:
  - YOLOX-S, Megvii (Apache-2.0)
  - MobileSAM, Chaoning Zhang et al. (Apache-2.0), converted to ONNX
- Packaged with PyInstaller (GPL-2.0 with bootloader exception) and
  Inno Setup.
